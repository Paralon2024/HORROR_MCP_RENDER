# Horror 2D Cartoon Style Rules
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical (720x1280 on Render Free Tier, 1080x1920 local/paid).
- **Color Palette:** Deep midnight shadows (#0A0C12), sickly phosphor monitor green (#0C2A20), blood-red captions and jump-scare flashes (#EB232D).
- **Available Backgrounds:** `corridor`, `security_room`, `woods`.
- **Night Guard Expressions:** `neutral`, `nervous`, `confused`, `shocked`, `shouting`, `possessed`.
- **Shadow Stalker Poses:** `hidden`, `lurking`, `peeking`, `lunging`.
- **Camera Moves:** `zoom_in`, `zoom_out`.
- **Screen FX:** `none`, `shake`, `flash_red`.
- **Audio SFX:** `none`, `heartbeat`, `stinger`.
